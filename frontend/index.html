About ORCA — Marine Ecosystem Reasoning with Collaborative Agents

ORCA is an agentic artificial intelligence platform designed for India's 4.5+ million coastal fisherfolk and maritime authorities. It synthesizes real-time ISRO Oceansat-3 satellite data, INCOIS oceanographic advisories, and marine telemetry to provide zero-hallucination fisheries navigation, proactive hazard warnings, and sovereign geofencing.

account_tree How ORCA Works: End-to-End Autonomous Pipeline

1 Data Ingestion

Continuous ingestion of Oceansat-3 satellite telemetry, Open-Meteo wave spectra, and coastal meteorological observations.

2 Spatial Analysis

Haversine distance, true compass bearing (0–360°), and Shapely polygon containment evaluate boundary hazards and fuel requirements.

3 Agentic Reasoning

Collaborative AI sub-agents synthesize findings into structured, culturally-tuned advisories across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali.

4 Multi-Modal Output

Instant delivery via interactive Leaflet charts, proactive top alert banners, and VHF/speech-ready text-to-speech engine.
