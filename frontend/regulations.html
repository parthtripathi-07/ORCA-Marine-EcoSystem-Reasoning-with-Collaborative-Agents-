ZERO RETENTION TOLERANCE
🦭 SCHEDULE I

Dugong / Sea Cow

Dugong dugon

Found in Gulf of Mannar, Palk Bay, and Gulf of Kutch. Completely banned from hunting, capture, or possession.

🐢 SCHEDULE I

Olive Ridley Sea Turtle

Lepidochelys olivacea

Mass nesting in Odisha & Chennai coasts. Mandatory installation of Turtle Excluder Devices (TED) in trawl nets.

🦈 SCHEDULE I

Whale Shark

Rhincodon typus

Found along Gujarat, Kerala, and Lakshadweep. Cut nets if entangled; government provides net compensation scheme.

🗡️ SCHEDULE I

Knifetooth / Largetooth Sawfish

Pristis pristis

Critically endangered ray species. Any trading, landing, or cutting of rostrum (saw blade) is strictly illegal.

🦪 SCHEDULE I

Giant Clams & Sea Cucumbers

Tridacna spp. / Holothuria spp.

Key coral reef ecosystem cleaners. Commercial harvesting in Tamil Nadu & Lakshadweep is totally forbidden.

🫧 SCHEDULE I

Seahorses & Pipefish

Hippocampus spp.

Dried seahorse export to international traditional medicine markets is banned under CITES and Wildlife Act.

grid_on Minimum Legal Net Mesh Size Regulations

Trawl Net Cod-End: 35 mm (Square Mesh)

Prevents trapping juvenile finfish and undersized bycatch.

Shrimp Trawl Net: 25 mm Minimum

Must be operated with a Turtle Excluder Device (TED) window.

Pelagic Gill Nets: 100 - 150 mm

For large pelagic species like Seerfish and Yellowfin Tuna.
